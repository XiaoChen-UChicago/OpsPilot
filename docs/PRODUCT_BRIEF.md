# Product brief

## Problem

Small teams frequently research companies in browser tabs, score opportunities in spreadsheets, draft messages in a separate AI tool, and update a CRM manually. The work is repetitive, and the reasoning behind prioritization is easily lost.

## User

The initial user is a founder, growth lead, operations lead, or strategy analyst at an early-stage B2B company with a manageable list of target accounts and no dedicated revenue-operations team.

## Job to be done

When I receive a new list of potential customers or partners, help me turn incomplete company data into a prioritized, reviewable pipeline so that I can spend my time on the opportunities most likely to matter.

## Product decisions

### Use visible scoring rules

The first version uses a transparent heuristic rather than a black-box prediction. At small data volumes, the main product need is consistent decision support and explicit assumptions.

### Keep outreach in review mode

Automated sending can amplify factual errors and poor targeting. OpsPilot produces drafts and context, while the user remains responsible for verification and sending.

### Work without external credentials

A portfolio reviewer should be able to run the full workflow immediately. The core product therefore works with synthetic data and deterministic business rules.

## Success metrics

- time from CSV upload to prioritized account list;
- percentage of high-priority accounts with complete evidence;
- percentage of outreach drafts approved without factual correction;
- number of manual research and copy-paste steps removed;
- user agreement with the top-ranked accounts.

## Known limitations

- Public homepages rarely provide reliable employee counts or buyer intent.
- Keyword signals can miss industry language or overvalue marketing copy.
- Draft personalization is constrained by the available evidence.
- The local CRM is intended for demonstration and small workflows.
